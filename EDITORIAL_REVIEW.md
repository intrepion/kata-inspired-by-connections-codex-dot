# Puzzle editorial review

Reviewed all 365 boards in source order on 29 October 2026. The review read each category and its four answers for obvious factual errors, clue fit, and possible competing groupings. It is an editorial pass by the author, not independent playtesting or proof that every alternate interpretation has been found. Difficulty ranks are estimates rather than calibrated player data.

The pass clarified several category labels without changing answer tiles, including dried fruits, cloud formations, gathering, deception, soft or halting speech, measured quantities, and ground/grounded. It also clarified that “blaze a trail” needs an article. Other corrections were made during earlier drafting. No existing save data was removed.

The automated integrity check confirms 365 unique board identities and word sets, four distinct words per group and ranks 1–4. A separate check rejects groups in different boards sharing three or more words. These checks do not assess semantics or all possible alternative groupings. Several boards use broad categories, uncommon vocabulary, or compound-word fragments; player testing is still needed to assess fairness and difficulty.
